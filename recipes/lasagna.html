Chicken and Spinach Alfredo Lasagna

Description

This is a delicious chicken and spinach Alfredo lasagna recipe that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 package lasagna noodles
  • 3 cups heavy cream
  • 2 cans condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 table spoon olive oil
  • 4 cloves gralic, 5 mushrooms diced
  • 1 cooked rotisseri chicken, shredded
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 bunch fresh spinach, rinsed
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix heavy cream of mushroom soup, parmesan cheese, and butter in a saucepan over low heat; simmer, stirring, frequently, until well blended
  3. Heat Olive oil in a skillet over medium heat., add garlic and mushrooms
  4. Lightly coat the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish with the cream sauce mixture
  5. Layer with 1/3 of the lasagna noodles, 1/2 cup ricotta, 1/2 of spinach
  6. Add 1/2 the chicken mixture, and 1 cup mozzarella cheese. Top with 1/3 the cream sauce mixture and repeat the layers
  7. Place the remaining noodles on top and spread with remaining sauce
  8. Bake 1 hour in the preheated oven, or until brown and bubbly
